It drafts a specific RTI, pre-filled with exact document IDs. A specific one cannot be refused for vagueness. It never names an individual. The target is the process.

Then it asks Lakshmi — who walks past the spot twice a day.

Every reporting system in India starts with the citizen: here is a form, tell us what’s wrong. PRAMAAN प्रमाण inverts it. The machine does the analysis first, then asks one physical question a person with no literacy, no training and no smartphone can answer in one syllable.

Lakshmi, 42

Kalaburagi, Karnataka. Works under MGNREGA when work is available; has a job card. Cannot read English, reads Kannada slowly, does not own a smartphone. Her son does, and he works in Bengaluru.

She is the highest-resolution sensor in the entire system, and nobody has ever asked her a question.

Her anonymous “no pond here” is not a complaint.

It is a ground-truth training label: the exact thing the procurement-fraud literature says does not exist. It retrains the network detector, so similar patterns elsewhere now score higher. More reports → a better model → better-targeted questions → more useful reports.

This closed loop is the novel contribution. Nobody has published it.

The honest limits, shipped with the capability, not in a footnote.

Overclaiming loses a technical judge and hurts people. So each capability arrives with the line that constrains it.

  • ⚠

    Satellite cannot see small assets

    At 10 m ground sample distance, one pixel is 100 m². Rural toilets, hand-pumps and single house extensions are physically invisible. This is a physical limit, not a modelling one. Small assets skip satellite entirely and go straight to a citizen.

  • ⚠

    Speech recognition is genuinely imperfect

    Best achievable word-error is 16.2% in Hindi and 35.1% in Odia: roughly one word in three. So VAANI never takes free-form dictation. Structured slots, DTMF fallback on every prompt, verbal confirmation loops.

  • ⚠

    This prototype dataset is synthetic

    The bundled corpus is a synthetic reconstruction of published CAG findings, not live-scraped portal data. Live ingest crawls the real public portals and is out of scope for a zero-network demo. Everything marked VALIDATED re-derives a real CAG number.

  • ⚠

    Read-only, and never one score

    It never denies a payment, blocks a job card, or gates a ration. And it emits four independent signals — never a fused “corruption score,” which the red-flag literature shows is statistically unjustified. A false positive costs an official an explanation, never a citizen her rice.
